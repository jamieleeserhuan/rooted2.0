How it works

  1. 1

    Tell us what you've done.

    Describe your work in your own words. Formal or informal, with or without documents.

  2. 2

    See where it fits.

    Rooted matches your experience to Canadian occupations.

  3. 3

    Get your pathway.

    A clear card with steps, time, cost, and funding you can apply for.

What you share stays with you.

  • Nothing is stored.

    Your words are never saved anywhere.

  • Nothing is logged.

    No account. No record of who you are or what you typed.

  • Gone when you close the page.

    Everything is deleted the moment you leave.

Who it's for

Rooted is for people who don't know where to start. For people who feel too overwhelmed to reach out yet. For people whose work never came with a certificate.

Rooted meets you where you are.

About

Rooted is an independent, open-source project. It was built for one reason: so anyone starting over in Canada can see where their experience fits, for free. The code is open, so anyone can read it, check it, and help make it better.
